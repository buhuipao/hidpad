# HidPad privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

HidPad has no ads, no account, and no analytics. It has no internet permission and makes no
network requests.

- **Bluetooth.** HidPad uses Bluetooth to act as a keyboard and mouse. The keys, pointer moves,
  and media keys you enter are sent to the computer you paired, and nowhere else. The app reads
  the list of paired devices to show it on the Bluetooth devices screen.
- **Camera.** The camera is used only on the Code scanner screen, to read QR codes and barcodes.
  Images are processed on the phone and are not saved. The text of a scanned code is typed on the
  paired computer, and the last 20 codes are shown on screen until you leave the screen.
- **Kept on the phone.** Three settings (pointer speed, scroll direction, Enter after a scan)
  and the Bluetooth address of the last connected computer, so the app can reconnect to it.
  They are not included in Android backups. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app
  removes them.
